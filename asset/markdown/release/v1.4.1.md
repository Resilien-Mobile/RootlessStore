# 启动体验修复版

Welcome to Rootless Store v1.4.1!

This release focuses on the app launch experience. In v1.4.1, Rootless Store improves the startup transition and fixes splash screen visual inconsistencies, making the first entry into the app feel cleaner and less abrupt.

欢迎来到 Rootless Store v1.4.1！

这个版本主要聚焦于应用启动体验。v1.4.1 优化了启动过渡，并修复了启动页相关的视觉不一致问题，让首次进入应用时的观感更干净，也不再那么突兀。

---

### What's New in v1.4.1

* **Improved Startup Experience**:
    * Added a more complete splash screen experience.
    * Improved the transition from app launch to the main interface.
* **Splash Screen Visual Fixes**:
    * Fixed splash icon background display inconsistencies on newer Android versions.
    * Improved theme consistency during app startup.

### v1.4.1 更新内容

* **启动体验优化**:
    * 新增了更完整的启动页体验。
    * 优化了从应用启动到主界面的过渡。
* **启动页视觉修复**:
    * 修复了较新 Android 版本上启动图标背景显示不一致的问题。
    * 提升了启动阶段的主题一致性。

---

### Bug Fixes & Experience Polish

* Fixed startup visuals that could feel inconsistent with the rest of the app.
* Improved the first-launch impression, especially on devices with newer Android splash behavior.
* The `1.4.x` series will continue focusing on these detail-level “obsessive” bug fixes and polish.

### Bug 修复与体验打磨

* 修复了启动阶段与应用整体视觉不够一致的问题。
* 改善了首次打开应用时的观感，尤其是较新 Android 版本上的启动页表现。
* 后续 `1.4.x` 版本会继续修复这类“强迫症”级别的细节 Bug，并持续打磨体验。
